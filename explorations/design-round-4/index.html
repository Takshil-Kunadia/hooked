Chapter 1 · The journey of a request

What happens when you visit a WordPress site?

⚠ your filter returned nothing, so the title is gone. That's the #1 filter bug.

Every page visit is a trip. Ride along with one request as it travels through WordPress, stop by stop, and comes back as a web page.

New to websites? Start at Stop 0 →

STOP 4 · init · do_action( 'init' )

This is an action.

  1. The train pulls into init. By now WordPress has loaded your plugins and theme, and it knows who the visitor is.
  2. WordPress makes an announcement. Think of a town-hall bell: do_action( 'init' ) calls out “init is happening now!” to everyone who signed up with add_action.
  3. Each listener takes its turn, by ticket number. The ticket is the priority: lowest goes first. Actions don't hand anything back. Each listener just does its job.

STOP 7 · the_title · apply_filters( 'the_title', $title )

This is a filter.

  1. The train carries a value. Before your theme prints a post title, the title rides through the the_title workshop.
  2. wptexturize polishes the typography. The straight apostrophe in isn't becomes a curly one: isn’t.
  3. capital_P_dangit fixes the spelling. “Wordpress” becomes “WordPress”. Yes, core really does this.
  4. A plugin adds its own touch. Every station gets the latest value, changes it (or not) and must hand it back.
  5. The finished value rolls out. That's what your theme prints.
Forget to hand it back
and the next station gets nothing (null).

Style tile · round 4

The Metro line

What changed since round 3

No signposts. Station names are printed on the map, tilted slightly toward you. The hero shows only station names, and the extra labels appear only at the stop they belong to.
A crisp line. An extruded, flat-topped ribbon with rounded ends replaces the soft tube.
Transit markers. White pills with an ink outline, like a real metro map. The current hook's station is outlined in blue.
Ticket passes. Listeners are upright passes showing their priority. On their turn, a pass lifts and flips to its colour with a check mark.
Portals and a data vault. The workshop gantries are arches with a glowing rim, and the depot is a porcelain vault with light slits.
